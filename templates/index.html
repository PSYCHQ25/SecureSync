How SecureSync Works Under the Hood

1
Data Collection
Curated dataset of real-world SMS and WhatsApp scams and normal messages.
2
Data Preprocessing
Normalizing case, tokenizing URLs, phones, and currency tokens.
3
NLP Features
TF-IDF Vectorization converting words and bigrams into weighted numerical vectors.
4
Model Training
Supervised classification comparing Logistic Regression and Naive Bayes.
5
Model Testing
Evaluation on unseen test data measuring Precision, Recall, and F1-score.
6
Scam Detection
Hybrid inference combining statistical ML with 7 heuristic indicator rules.
7
User Alert
Actionable threat advisory, confidence score, and quarantined link inspection.

Scam Tactics & System Limitations

Artificial Urgency: Scammers simulate fake crises ("account suspended within 2 hours") to bypass logical skepticism.
Authority & Fear: Impersonating IRS, sheriff, or bank fraud desks to induce panic compliance.
Adversarial Evasion: Attackers often alter spellings ("w1nner", "cl!ck") or use brand new domains.
Important Disclaimer: Machine learning models are probabilistic. A prediction of "Legitimate" does not guarantee absolute safety. Always verify sensitive requests out-of-band through official phone numbers.
