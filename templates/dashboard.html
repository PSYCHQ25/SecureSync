Scam Tactics & System Safeguards

Artificial Urgency: Scammers simulate fake crises ("account suspended within 2 hours", "power disconnect") to bypass logical skepticism.
Authority & Intimidation: Impersonating IRS, sheriff, or bank fraud teams to induce panic compliance.
Zero-Trust Link Quarantine: Web addresses are defanged (hxxp://) and non-clickable to protect security analysts and students from malicious payload delivery.
Important Disclaimer: Machine learning models are probabilistic. A prediction of "Legitimate" does not guarantee absolute safety. Always verify sensitive requests out-of-band through official phone numbers.
